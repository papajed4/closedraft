Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! You can start with the Free plan. Upgrade to Pro anytime when you need more clients and emails.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with CloseDraft.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade to Pro or downgrade to Free anytime from your account settings.

What happens when I hit the free limits?

You'll see a friendly upgrade prompt. Your data stays safe—you just can't add more clients or generate more emails until you upgrade.
