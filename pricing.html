Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — no contracts, no commitments. You can cancel your Pro plan at any time through the Polar Customer Portal (link in your purchase receipt email). Your account will revert to the Free plan at the end of your billing period, and your data stays safe.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! The Free plan lets you manage up to 10 clients and generate 15 AI‑powered emails per month — no credit card required. Upgrade to Pro whenever you need unlimited clients, AI emails, Gmail integration, AutoDraft campaigns, and Discord/Telegram notifications.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. We offer a 30‑day money‑back guarantee on annual Pro plans. If you're not satisfied within the first month, email us and we'll refund you in full. Monthly plans are non‑refundable but you can cancel anytime to stop future charges.

How do I manage my subscription?

After purchasing a Pro plan, you'll receive an email from Polar.sh (our payment partner). Inside that email is a link to your Polar Customer Portal. From there you can upgrade, downgrade, switch between monthly and yearly billing, update your payment method, or cancel your subscription — all self‑serve, no support tickets needed.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade from Free to Pro anytime — your data, clients, emails, and templates stay exactly as they are. If you ever need to downgrade back to Free, you'll keep everything but Pro‑only features (unlimited clients, Gmail integration, AutoDraft, Discord/Telegram) will be paused until you re‑subscribe.

What happens when I hit the free limits?

When you reach 10 clients or 15 AI emails in a month, you'll see a friendly upgrade prompt in the dashboard. Your existing data stays safe — you just won't be able to add new clients or generate more AI emails until the next month or until you upgrade to Pro for unlimited access.
