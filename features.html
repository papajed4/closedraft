Powerful features for busy freelancers

CloseDraft combines client tracking, AI‑powered email writing, automated follow‑ups, and real‑time notifications — so you never lose a deal to a forgotten email again.

Client Hub

All your clients, projects, payment amounts, and conversations in one dashboard. Track status, deadlines, and last contact at a glance.

AI Email Assistant

Generate personalized follow-ups, payment reminders, and cold outreach in seconds. Choose your tone — Friendly, Professional, Firm, or Casual. Two distinct subject lines every time.

Needs Attention Alerts

CloseDraft shows exactly which clients need you today — stale contacts, overdue deadlines, and payment reminders. Never forget a follow‑up again.

AutoDraft

Set‑and‑forget follow‑ups and campaigns. One‑click single sequences or bulk outreach. Emails are sent automatically via Gmail, with progress tracking and reply detection that stops campaigns once the lead responds.

Gmail Sync

Connect your Gmail and let CloseDraft send automated follow‑ups and sequences directly from your inbox. Reply detection ensures no client gets over‑emailed.

Discord & Telegram Alerts

Get real‑time client notifications where you work — private Discord channels or Telegram bot. Customise which events trigger a ping.

Invoice Generator

Turn client project data into a professional PDF invoice with one click. Auto‑generated numbers and a polished layout — ready to print or email.

Client Activity Timeline

See every interaction — emails sent, status changes, and contact marks — in a permanent, scrollable history inside each client's detail panel.

Templates Library

Save your best emails and reuse them with one click. AI will automatically personalise them for each client.

Import & Export

Bulk import clients via CSV. Export your complete client list anytime — your data stays yours, always.
